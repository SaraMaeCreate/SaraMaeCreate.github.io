Arnold Lumber's 2026 Hot Wing Challenge

The Goal: Create a cohesive promotional campaign that would build excitement around the Arnold Lumber Hot Wings Challenge, encourage contestant registration and event attendance. Ultimately, maximize fundraising in support of the Gloria Gemma Breast Cancer Resource Foundation.

The Results: The campaign helped drive the most successful Hot Wings Challenge in company history.

Gilbane Employee Resource Groups Brand Initiative

The Goal: Develop distinct visual identities for seven employee resource groups that reflected each group's mission, culture, and values while maintaining consistency with Gilbane's established brand standards.

The Results: Partnered directly with seven employee resource groups to create custom identities that reflected their unique communities while strengthening their connection to the broader Gilbane brand. The final logo system was adopted across the organization and provided each group with a recognizable and consistent visual identity for communications, events, and member engagement.

From Inaugural ESG Report to Flagship Corporate Publication

From establishing the visual identity of the inaugural Gilbane Cares Report (Environmental, Social, and Governance) to integrating a company-wide brand refresh, developing scalable iconography and infographic systems, and improving interactivity year after year, my focus was not on reinventing the publication, but on continually refining it. The result was a flagship corporate report that evolved alongside the company while turning complex ideas, meaningful data, and diverse perspectives into a cohesive visual story.
