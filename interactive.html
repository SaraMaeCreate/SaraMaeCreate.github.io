Gilbane Impact Report

As the lead designer for Gilbane, Inc.’s 2024 Impact Report, I was responsible for the complete layout and visual presentation of this ESG-inspired publication. Using Adobe InDesign, I developed a cohesive design system that aligned with Gilbane’s brand identity while including custom infographics and visual elements to enhance storytelling and data visualization. The final product communicates complex Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) initiatives in a clear and engaging way.
